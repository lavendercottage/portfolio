# Welcome

This portfolio is a Docusaurus-based home for work, ideas and hands-on learning. It is designed to grow as new case studies, projects, and experiences are added.

## Site structure

My aim is to create a simple and easy-to-navigate site where you can explore:

* **Docs-as-Code** – An introduction to the concept and workflow.
* **Documentation examples** – A mix of fictional examples and practical guides you can follow and try for yourself.
* **Information Design** – Reflections on how to structure documentation, concepts on visual design, and thoughts on how good UX can improve usability and experience.


>**This site is best experienced using the navigation sidebar**

## How to navigate

Whether you're using a desktop computer, tablet, or mobile device, the navigation is designed to help you find information quickly.

### On desktop

On larger screens, you'll find the navigation menu in the left-hand sidebar. Use it to move between topics and articles.

On the right hand side of the page, you will find a table of contents (similar to word chapters). Use it to jump directly to a specific section without scrolling through the entire document.

### On mobile

On smaller screens, the navigation menu is hidden to maximise reading space.

Tap the **menu icon (☰)** to open the documentation sidebar. From there, you can browse sections and jump directly to another page.
